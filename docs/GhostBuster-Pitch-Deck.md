## Slide 2 — Problem & Insight

### The problem

Students meet dozens of people at career fairs, coffee chats, and interviews — then lose momentum.

- Contacts live in notes apps, LinkedIn threads, and memory
- Follow-ups feel awkward: *“What do I say? Has it been too long?”*
- Good intentions fade; relationships go cold
- Opportunities slip to people who **stay in touch**, not just who **met once**

### Our insight

> **Your career isn’t shaped by who you meet — it’s shaped by who you stay in touch with.**

LinkedIn optimizes for discovery and visibility.  
**GhostBuster optimizes for follow-through.**

## Slide 4 — Solution (One-liner + workflow)

### GhostBuster AI

**A networking assistant that helps you foster and maintain meaningful professional connections** — from crafting the perfect first message to keeping those relationships warm over time.

### User workflow

1. **Meet someone** — career fair, coffee chat, referral, interview  
2. **Save the contact** — role, company, notes, how you know them  
3. **Capture context** — Ghostwriter live notes during online meetings → AI summary  
4. **Follow up** — AI Compose drafts warm outreach; Gmail draft/schedule  
5. **Stay consistent** — Tracker logs touchpoints; Reminders + Google Calendar sync  
6. **Grow with your career** — Résumé updates → AI suggests who to notify  

**GhostBuster doesn’t find strangers for you. It helps you nurture people you’ve already met.**

## Slide 6 — Differentiation

### vs. LinkedIn

| | LinkedIn | GhostBuster AI |
|---|----------|----------------|
| **Core job** | Be found; grow network | **Keep relationships warm** |
| **Starts after** | Connection request | **After you actually talk** |
| **Context** | Profile + DM thread | Meeting notes, touchpoint history, résumé fit |
| **Follow-up** | Blank message box | AI drafts + reminders + cross-channel tracker |
| **Success metric** | Connections, views | **Consistent, thoughtful outreach** |

### vs. generic CRM / Notion

- Built for **students**, not sales teams  
- **AI-native** compose + meeting summaries  
- Low setup — one place from “nice to meet you” to “following up”  

### Moat (building)

- Habit + contact graph + conversation memory  
- Campus distribution + career-center partnerships  
- AI tuned for **warm** student networking (not cold spam)

## Appendix — Speaker notes (optional)

### Slide 2 talking point
“Everyone talks about networking like it’s about meeting more people. We think the bottleneck is **ghosting** — letting good connections die because follow-up is hard and scattered.”

### Slide 4 talking point
Walk through a 60-second demo: add contact from career fair → Ghostwriter after Zoom coffee chat → Compose draft → log in Tracker → set reminder.

### Slide 6 talking point
“We’re not trying to replace LinkedIn. We’re the **layer after the handshake** — the anti-ghosting infrastructure.”

### Objection handling
- **“Is this just a CRM?”** — CRMs are built for sales quotas; we’re built for students and warm relationships.  
- **“Will students pay?”** — Land with free habit; monetize power users and institutions.  
- **“AI-generated messages feel fake?”** — User edits every draft; GhostBuster adds context from *their* notes, not generic templates.
